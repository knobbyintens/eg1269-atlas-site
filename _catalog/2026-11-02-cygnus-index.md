---
title: "Cygnus-Index: Position-Aware Index Maintenance under Drifting Embeddings"
kind: paper
announced_at: 2026-11-02
venue: "KDD 2027"
---
Cygnus-Index is a position-aware index structure for approximate nearest neighbour search on drifting embeddings.  We analyse how segment compaction interacts with recall and describe a maintenance policy that bounds index size under continuous ingestion.
