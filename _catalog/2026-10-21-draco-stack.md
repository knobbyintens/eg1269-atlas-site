---
title: "Draco-Stack: Co-locating Latency-Critical and Batch Inference"
kind: paper
announced_at: 2026-10-21
venue: "MLSys 2027"
---
Draco-Stack explores vertically integrated model serving for multi-tenant inference workloads.  We describe a scheduling layer that co-locates latency-critical and batch traffic while preserving tail latency, together with the capacity model used for planning.
