announced_at: 2026-09-04
venue: "NeurIPS 2026 Datasets and Benchmarks Track"
---
We present Helios, a benchmark suite for short-horizon solar irradiance nowcasting.  The suite standardises three public measurement networks, a fixed evaluation protocol and a set of reference baselines.  Helios is intended to make cross-paper comparisons reproducible without requiring authors to redistribute raw sensor data.
